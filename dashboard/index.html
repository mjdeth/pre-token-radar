Pre-Token Radar

Protocols that record no token yet, ranked by what a launch could be worth — and how to farm each one before it happens.

How a value gets on this board

  1. Comparables. For each category, take the protocols whose tokens already trade and find their median market-cap to TVL ratio, shrunk toward the global median when a category has few traded names. Cut the same traded set by TVL size and the pattern is steep: big protocols trade at far lower multiples than small ones.
  2. Base value. A pre-token protocol's base value is its TVL times a blend of its category ratio and its size-band ratio, each cut weighted by how many traded protocols stand behind it, so a one-name category can inform a price but never carry it. No name is hardcoded; every figure comes from the snapshot itself, and both comparable tables are published below.
  3. Adjustments. Seven-day TVL momentum, years run with no token, and chain reach each nudge the base up or down. Every row's detail view shows its own three factors.
